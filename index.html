Four kinds of wild

Mountains

High country

Alpine meadows, glaciers and sharp ridgelines. Pack layers, because the weather changes fast.

Forests

Old growth

Tall, quiet woods where the light comes down in beams. Perfect for slow, shady walks.

Lakes

Still water

Mirror-like lakes with mountain views. Go at sunrise when the surface is calm.

Deserts

Red rock

Stone arches, canyons and huge skies. Bring more water than you think you need.

Before you go

Check the weather

Conditions in the park can differ a lot from the nearest town.

Carry the basics

Water, a map, snacks, a light and a charged phone.

Leave no trace

Pack out what you pack in and stay on marked trails.
